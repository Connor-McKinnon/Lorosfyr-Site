Services and Shops

This page contains information on items purchasable from shops!

Blackmount Citadel

Location: Atop the Black Mountains.

Special Bonuses: ~25% discount on 50+ gp items (does not include special services)

Standard Equipment (PHB):

  • All weapons
  • All armor
  • All adventuring gear
  • All tools
  • Standard mounts

Special Equipment:

  • Potion of healing (40 gp)

Special Services (PHB):

  • Silver a weapon or 10 pieces of ammunition (100 gp, takes 1 hour)

Vyrmas

Location: Beneath the Catacombs, near the Fumewoods.

Special Bonuses: ~25% discount on 50+ gp items (does not include special services)

Standard Equipment (PHB):

  • All weapons
  • All armor
  • All adventuring gear
  • All tools

Special Equipment:

  • Potion of healing (40 gp)
  • Potion of greater healing (100 gp)
  • Driftglobe (150 gp)

Special Services (PHB):

  • Silver a weapon or 10 pieces of ammunition (100 gp, takes 1 hour)
  • Enchant a weapon, becomes +1 weapon (200 gp, takes 2 hours)

Silverlight Sanctuary

Location: In the Fumewoods within an enchanted mist.

Special Bonuses: None.

Standard Equipment (PHB):

  • Simple weapons
  • Armor 75 gp or less
  • Adventuring gear 50 gp or less except healing potions
  • All tools

Special Services (PHB):

  • Silver a weapon or 10 pieces of ammunition (100 gp, takes 1 hour)
