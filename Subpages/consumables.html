Consumable Items

This page contains consumable item descriptions!

Elemental Gem

Uncommon

  • You can use an action to break the gem, summoning an elemental as if you had cast conjure elemental. You must still maintain concentration.
  • The elemental summoned is determined by the type of gem as described below.
  • Red corundum. Summon a fire elemental (CR 5).

Elixir of Darkblood

Rare

  • You gain the Darkblood Infection condition for 24 hours, at which point you are safely restored to your original state.
  • When you return to your normal state, you are incapacitated for 1d6+12 hours due to your body violently expelling the Darkblood fluids.
  • If you already have the Darkblood Infection condition, you instead regain 40 hp.

Potion of Giant Strength

Varies

  • When you drink this potion, your Strength score becomes a set value for 1 hour unless it is already higher.
  • Hill Giant. 21 Strength (+5). (150 gp)
  • Frost/Stone Giant. 23 Strength (+6). (250 gp)
  • Fire Giant. 25 Strength (+7).

Potion of Healing

Varies

  • You can drink this potion to regain hit points. You can drink it normally as a bonus action, or you can fully consume it as an action to regain the maximum amount of hit points.
  • Potion of Healing. Regain 2d4+2 (10) hp. (50 gp)
  • Potion of Greater Healing. Regain 4d4+4 (20) hp. (125 gp)
  • Potion of Superior Healing. Regain 8d8+8 (40) hp. (300 gp)
  • Other variants:
    • Adrenaline Potion of Healing. Regain 2d4+2 (10) hp, your speed increases by 10 feet until the end of your turn.

Potion of Heroism

Rare

  • For 1 hour after drinking this potion, you gain 10 temporary hit points.
  • For the same duration, you are under the effects of the bless spell (no concentration required).

Potion of Invulnerability

Rare

  • When you drink this potion, you gain resistance to all damage for 1 minute.

Potion of Resistance

Uncommon

  • When you drink this potion, you gain resistance to the damage type specified when you obtain it.

Quaal's Feather Token, Whip

Rare

  • You can use your action to throw this token up to 10 feet. The token becomes a floating whip for 1 hour. You can then immediately make an attack with the whip using your bonus action.
  • Attack: +9 to hit, 1d6+5 force damage, reach 10 feet.
  • You can use your bonus action on subsequent turns to move the whip up to 20 feet and cause it to make another attack.
  • The whip disappears after 1 hour, when you use an action to dismiss it, or when you are incapacitated or die.

Shadow Coin

Rare

  • If you or a creature you can see fail a saving throw, you can use your reaction to flip this coin. If you successfully predict the coin flip, the target instead succeeds on their saving throw. Whether or not the coin flip succeeds, the coin then disappears.

Vial of Darkblood

Uncommon

  • You can drink this as an action or bonus action. You gain 10 temporary hit points from Darkblood, or regain 10 hit points if you have Darkblood Infection.
