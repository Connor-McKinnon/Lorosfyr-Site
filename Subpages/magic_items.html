Magic Items

This page contains non-consumable magic item descriptions!

Amulet of the Devout (+1)

Uncommon, requires attunement by a cleric or paladin

Bearer: Lyrix

  • While wearing this holy symbol, you gain a +1 bonus to your spell attack rolls and your spell save DC.
  • Once per day, you can use your Channel Divinity for free.

Brooch of Shielding

Uncommon, requires attunement

Bearer: Party

  • You gain resistance to force damage.
  • You are immune to damage from magic missile.

Blood Pendant (+2)

This pendant has 4 charges. It regains all charges when you finish a long rest. Once per turn, you may expend its charges to use one of its abilities. (HD = your hit die, CON = your constitution modifier)

  • Healing (1-4). Action: you regain 2dHD hp per charge expended + 2×CON hp.
  • Fast Healing (1). Bonus Action: you regain 2dHD + 2×CON hp.
  • Revive (1). While at 0 hp, an ally can use their action to cause you to regain 2dHD + 2×CON hp.
  • Recharge. You may spend hit dice without regaining hp during a short rest. The pendant regains a charge for every 2 hit dice spent in this way. These hit dice may now be of any size you have.

Cape of the Mountebank

Rare

Bearer: Melchor

  • Teleportation. The wearer of this cape can cast dimension door once per day.
  • Smoke Cloud. You leave behind a smoke cloud in the spaces where you disappear and appear; this smoke lightly obscures the spaces until the end of your next turn; a light wind disperses it.

Cloak of Elvenkind

Uncommon, requires attunement

Bearer: Melchor

  • While you wear this cloak with its hood up, Wisdom (Perception) checks made to see you have disadvantage, and you have advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made to hide.

Cloak of Protection

Uncommon, requires attunement

Bearer: Sebastian

  • You gain a +1 bonus to AC and saving throws while you wear this cloak.

Crown of Nyxlanyss

Uncommon, requires attunement

Bearer: Hark

  • Your Intelligence score becomes 19 (+4) unless it is already higher.

Dancing Scimitar

Very Rare, requires attunement

Bearer: Sebastian

  • You can use a bonus action to toss this magic sword into the air and speak the command phrase, "dance and slice." It begins to hover and flies up to 30 feet, attacking one creature within 5 feet of it, using your attack roll and ability score modifier to damage rolls.
  • While hovering, you can use a bonus action to cause it to fly up to 30 feet to another spot within 30 feet of you, causing it to attack a creature within 5 feet of it after flying.
  • Once it has attacked four times, it attempts to fly 30 feet back to your hand, falling to the ground if it can't reach you or if you can't catch it. It also stops hovering if you move more than 30 feet away from it or if you grasp it.

Finger of Magic Missiles

Uncommon

Bearer: Hark

  • This wand has 7 charges. It regains 1d6+1 charges at dawn.
  • The wand can cast magic missle for 1 charge. Each charge expended above 1 increases the level by 1.
  • If you expend the wand's last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the wand crumbles into ashes and is destroyed.

Forbidden Tome

Very Rare, requires attunement by a non-good creature

Bearer: Hark

  • Quick Cast. A number of times per day equal to your proficiency bonus (3), you can cast a spell as a bonus action if it normally has a casting time of 1 action.
  • Summon Demon. Once per day, you can cast summon greater demon without a spell slot. When you cast it in this way, you gain a +2 to your save DC for this spell.

Gauntlets of Ogre Power

Uncommon, requires attunement

Bearer: Hark

  • Your Strength score is 19 while you wear these gauntlets.

Harkon's Skeletal Hand

Uncommon, requires attunement

Bearer: Drangea

  • While attuned, this skeletal hand fuses to the top of your right hand.
  • You gain proficiency in Intimidation while attuned to this hand. If you are already proficient, you instead gain expertise in Intimidation.
  • Your unarmed strikes are considered magical, and you gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls with unarmed strikes.
  • Once per day, you can use a bonus action to empower the hand. For 1 minute, Your melee attacks with weapons or unarmed strikes can reach targets up to 15 feet away as inky tendrils launch toward the target. Additionally, these attacks deal an exra 1d6 force damage on a hit.

Mace of Disruption

Rare, requires attunement

Bearer: Lyrix

  • When you hit a fiend or an undead with this mace, that creature takes an extra 2d6 radiant damage.
  • If the target has 25 hp or fewer after taking this damage, it must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or be destroyed.
  • On a successful save, the creature is frightened of you until the end of your next turn.
  • While held, the mace sheds bright light in a 20-foot radius and dim light for an additional 20 feet.

Mithral Armor

Uncommon

Bearer: Lyrix

  • This armor does not impose disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.

Ring of Necrotic Resistance

Rare, requires attunement

Bearer: Drangea

  • You have resistance to necrotic damage while wearing this ring.
  • If you would gain levels of Dark Miasma, you instead gain half as many (rounded down).

Ring of Mind Shielding

Uncommon, requires attunement

Bearer: Sebastian

  • You are immune to magic that allows other creatures to read your thoughts, determine whether you are lying, know your alignment, or know your creature type.
  • Creatures can telepathically communicate with you only if you allow it.
  • You can use your action to cause the ring to become invisible until you use another action to make it visible, remove the ring, or die.
  • The ring contains the soul of Queen Dahlia. As long as her soul is in the ring, she can telepathically communicate with any creature wearing it. The wearer can't prevent this telepathic communication.

Ring of Protection

Uncommon, requires attunement

Bearer: Lyrix

  • You gain a +1 bonus to your Armor Class and saving throws.

Sending Stones

Uncommon

Bearer: Sebastian

  • Once per day, one of these stone's bearers can cast sending to the bearer of the other stone.

Spear of the Tanar'ri

Uncommon

Bearer: Lyrix

  • +1 Bonus. This spear has a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls.
  • Rays of Fire. This spear can be used to cast scorching ray with a +7 bonus once per day, regaining the ability to do so at dawn.
  • Icy Destruction. If this spear is submerged in a frigid river from sundown to sunrise, it is destroyed.

Talisman of Ultimate Evil

Legendary, requires attunement by an evil creature

Bearer: Hark

  • If a creature that is neutral or unaligned touches this talisman, that creature takes 6d6 necrotic damage.
  • If a creature that is good aligned touches this talisman, that creature takes 8d6 necrotic damage.
  • A creature attuned to this talisman can use it as a holy symbol, gaining a +2 bonus to spell attack rolls.
  • The talisman has 6 charges (5 remaining). You can expend a charge to target a good-aligned creature within 120 feet of you. If it fails a DC 20 Dexterity saving throw, it falls into a flaming fissure and is destroyed, leaving no trace or remains.
  • If the last charge is expended, the talisman will be destroyed.

Twisted Sword

Uncommon

Bearer: None

  • +1 Bonus. This longsword has a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls.
  • Life Drain. When you roll a natural 20 on an attack with this weapon, you gain 2d8 temporary hit points.
  • Backbite. When you roll a natural 1 on an attack with this weapon, you take 2d8 necrotic damage, and your hit point maximum is reduced by the same amount until you finish a long rest.
  • Sunlight Weakness. If the sword is exposed to sunlight for 1 minute, it crumbles and is destroyed.
