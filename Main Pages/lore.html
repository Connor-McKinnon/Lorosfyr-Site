Lorosfyr Lore

This page contains information on the different areas of Lorosfyr you've visisted as well as details about the Archon Pantheon (see below).

Blackmount Citadel

Depth: Surface

An old city atop the Black Mountains, the Blackmount Citadel is long past its prime. Once a bustling city and stop along the way into Lorosfyr, only the occasional traveler passes through, and many people have left for greener pastures. However, thanks to the party's overthrowing of Damon Vortex and establishment of Maximus as the new mayor, the city has started getting back on its feet.

Notable Characters:

  • Damon Vortex: Deceased. The former mayor of the citadel, overthrown by the party after a failed attempt to take the surrounding region by force with demonic aid.
  • Knight Maximus: An ally to the party, aided them in the rebellion against Mayor Vortex. Now leads the citadel as their new mayor.
  • Eustace: A historian familiar with documents regarding Lorosfyr, knowledgeable about locations within the caverns.

Forsaken Catacombs

Depth: 100 feet

Old catacombs which house the remains of the long deceased. The people of the Blackmount Citadel have left it abandoned, resulting in undead occasionally appearing unchallenged. Once the home of Omen Vortex's undead remains, the catacombs now lie mostly empty due to the party's passing through. Only the undead spirit of Violetta remains. The catacombs contain access to the elevator further down into Lorosfyr.

Notable Characters:

  • Omen Vortex: Deceased. Damon's father, sought to use Nyxeram's power to take over the surrounding region, was sealed away and eventually defeated by the party.
  • Violetta: Self-obsessed spirit residing within the Catacombs, mostly keeps to herself.

Vyrmas

Depth: 5 miles

The first town travelers pass through in Lorosfyr, Vyrmas acts as a supply stop before venturing further into the Fumewoods or the Mithral Foundry. As a relatively small town, supplies are limited, but passage between here and the Blackmount Citadel is decently common. Nearby locations include the tower of the witch Eris Nocturna and the Warrior's Crossroads where the 3 warrior sisters limit passage to those strong enough to survive the challenges ahead.

Notable Characters:

  • Rolen Mellowbutter: Mayor of Vyrmas, maintains order within the town. He and his soldiers have aided the party in their battle against Damon Vortex.
  • Father Alvin: Priest of Lathander in Vyrmas. He provided the party's first upgrade to their Blood Pendants, helped aid them against Damon Vortex with knowledge.
  • Eris Nocturna: Witch who resides within the Bloodcrest Spire, tinkers with magical constructs in solitude.
  • Warrior Sisters: Helara, Theris, and Mina. Sisters who battle with those who seek to progress further into Lorosfyr, only allowing passage to those strong enough to survive.

Fumewoods

Depth: 6 miles

A fungal swamp which block access further into Lorosfyr. Divided into the Fumewoods' Edge, the Twisting Darkwood, the Rotwood, the Bogwood, the Border Caves, and the Glittering Glades. Once the home of Agatha the night hag before the party dispatched of her. Briefly had an increase in Dark Miasma while Drangea was corrupted, also where Tenebris the Ancient Darkrender primarily lives. The Silverlight Sanctuary hides within the Fumewoods, a sanctuary for followers of Eilistraee.

Notable Characters:

  • Agatha: Deceased. A hag who used to steal the dreams of people while they slumbered, resulting in eventual death unless her victim made a bargain with her. Slain by the party after haunting Hark's dreams.
  • Michael the Rotlord: Deceased. An angel devoted to drawing in Agatha's rot, fell to Dark Miasma and was slain by the party in order to purify Drangea.
  • Priestess Valanthe: A priestess of Eilistraee who leads the Silverlight Sanctuary, aided the party in freeing Drangea from the Dark Miasma.
  • Tenebris the Ancient Darkrender: An ancient dragon who became more powerful after absorbing a substantial amount of Dark Miasma. She retains control over herself due to her immense power. One of her attacks resulted in the death of Aster. She mainly slumbers, but she is awakened by and drawn to increases in the level of nearby Dark Miasma.

Mithral Foundry

Depth: 6 miles

A mine and forge once used for the creation of Mithral armor, weapons, and tools, now abandoned. The foundry has not been passed through by the party. Acts as a shorter but more dangerous path than the Fumewoods. An outpost of soldiers were called from here by Ned to aid in the attack against Queen Dahlia.

Nyxlanyss

Depth: 16 miles

A city far below the surface of the world. Previously ruled by an evil monarchy, slowly rotting away. The party took action to rally the people and overthrow Queen Dahlia. After an extended campaign, they succeeded but unleashed a flood of Dark Miasma upon the city in the process, resulting in the city's population being almost entirely destroyed.

Notable Characters:

  • Queen Dahlia Nyxlanyss: Deceased. Queen of Nyxlanyss, worked to maintain her rule while suppressing rebellion. Worked against the party until her battle with them where she injected herself with a Dark Miasma concoction.
  • Ned Flammington: Deceased. Leader of the rebellion against the Queen, helped the party organize the people as part of a social movement; during the final attack, Nyxeram took control of him and forced the party to kill him as the city collapsed.
  • Melchor: Deceased. The Queen's wizard, a powerful spellcaster capable of 9th-level magic. Worked against the party, formed a concoction using Dark Miasma and Drangea's blood to empower the Queen, was slain by the party. Melchor's notes on Nyxeram
  • John Guinnessberg: Deceased. A slimy politician, worked as the Queen's financial advisor. Slain by the party so they could take his guise, and was used as a figurehead against the Queen.
  • Mintho: Deceased. An elven blacksmith, the last known person to have returned from the lost city of Mythrallar hundreds of years ago. Helped forged weapons and armor for the party, provided them the second upgrade to their Blood Pendants. Presumably died during the fall of the city.
  • Penna and Charta: Deceased. Cultists of Oghma, tasked Hark with seeking out the Song of Fading Light. Slain by Nyxeram, reanimated by their dark threads and slain again by the party.

Archon Pantheon

The Archons are powerful beings that take the place of gods in Lorosfyr. Although not divine, they are capable of great feats and can grant boons to their worshippers. The Archons do not adhere to mortal morality and take in followers both good and evil, so long as their domain is respected. The pantheon consists of the following:

  • Cernunnos, Archon of Nature
  • Malleator, Archon of Creation
  • Quetzalcoatl, Archon of Knowledge
  • Lycaon, Archon of Identity
  • The Morrigan, Archon of Battle
  • Osiris, Archon of Death
  • Nemesis, Archon of Balance

Cernunnos

Archon of Nature

Appearance: Appears as a man with various animal features, plant-made clothing, and earthy skin.

Personality: Cernunnos can be quite kind when nature is respected, but those who try to work against nature will face a wrath with the force of a natural disaster, often cursing them to become the parts of nature they seek to destroy.

Followers: Those who respect or rely upon nature commonly follow Cernunnos. This can include hunters, rangers, druids, and others who spend time outdoors.

Malleator

Archon of Creation

Appearance: Appears as a built man with various tools of craftsmanship and art.

Personality: Takes great pride in those who work hard, seeks to encourage people to follow creative passions and work hard. Scoffs at those who take credit for others' work and ideas and curses them with laziness and a lack of creativity.

Followers: Those who work hard towards their goals and make creative endeavors follow Malleator. This can include craftsmen, artists, inventors, and spellcasters.

Quetzalcoatl

Archon of Knowledge

Appearance: They appear as a feathered serpent with many scales and wings covered in miniscule writing.

Personality: They place the seeking of knowledge above all else, even to the point of obtaining knowledge that has no practical use. Favors those who seek to learn, whether for good or ill. Those who seek to prevent the seeking of knowledge are cursed by Quetzalcoatl with the inability to learn from their mistakes.

Followers: All who seek to gather and use knowledge may seek to follow Quetzalcoatl. This can include scholars, historians, and wizards.

Lycaon

Archon of Identity

Appearance: They appear as a wolf-like humanoid with fur of various shades of gray, black, and brown.

Personality: Lycaon's personality changes easily, shifting between extremes. They favor those seeking to become the person they are inside. They often magically reveal a person's true self, becoming beautiful and happy if good, or a monstrous creature if evil.

Followers: Those who seek to uncover their true selves seek out the aid of Lycaon. Lycaon's followers are more varied, coming from all aspects of life, but particularly those unhappy with who they are.

The Morrigan

Archon of Battle

Appearance: She appears as a tall warrior with a spiked spear and a raven-feather cloak.

Personality: The Morrigan favors those who battle for a greater cause, regardless of its morality. Those who fight selfishly or dishonorably are cursed with defeat after defeat, although a selfish goal is not inherently disqualifying. She values honor in battle, detesting those who disobey the rules of war.

Followers: Those who fight for a cause turn to the Morrigan for her favor. This can include soldiers, warriors, paladins, and anyone who frequently finds themselves in battle.

Osiris

Archon of Death

Appearance: He appears as a pale corpse stitched back together dressed in royal regalia with dark hollow eyes.

Personality: Osiris values the sanctity of death above all else, seeing it as a necessary part of life. Those who seek to challenge death via necromancy or immortality are cursed with the misery that eternal life can offer until they succumb to death's embrace. All who seek comfort from death or to give death to others gain his favor.

Followers: Very few remain constant followers of Osiris. Those in mourning may turn to Osiris. Constant followers include gravekeepers and assassins, as well as those who dispatch of the undead.

Nemesis

Archon of Balance

Appearance: She appears in a judge's robes with religious decorations, and is commonly seen holding the scales of justice.

Personality: Nemesis seeks to balance out a person's luck and fate. Those with great fortune are taken down a notch while those with poor fortune are given a break. Additionally, a person's actions, both good and bad, are returned to them in kind by her. Nemesis does not discriminate with who she favors or disfavors, nor is her favor or ire personal.

Followers: Nemesis' followers tend to be those down on their luck and those who want to see the powerful taken down a notch. More constant followers can include those who enforce justice on wrongdoers, but can also include those who seek societal equality.
